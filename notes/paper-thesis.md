# Frozen paper thesis

Extended-statistics physical design cannot be evaluated reliably from
independent candidate scores because PostgreSQL's estimator executes
configuration-dependent, statistics-sensitive state transitions. CE-Replay
preserves those transitions as a workload-specialized, design-parametric
executable representation, enabling faithful hypothetical cardinality
estimation, semantic/counterfactual dependency reasoning, and a deployable
offline statistics-advisor workflow.

This thesis does not claim a new CE algorithm, generic partial-evaluation
theory, or a global optimizer.
