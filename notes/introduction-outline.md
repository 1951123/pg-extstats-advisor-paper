   they alter optimizer information and maintenance work.
2. **Why singleton/static responses fail:** applicability, winner selection,
   consumption, MCV-to-FD composition, precedence, and payload updates make
   utility contextual and potentially non-monotone.
3. **Representation insight:** Preserve the statistics-sensitive estimator
   state transitions and keep design-dependent behavior executable.
4. **System architecture:** Separate stock production capture from offline
   hypothetical advice and DBA-controlled deployment/verification.
5. **Evidence summary:** RQ1 frozen/fresh fidelity, RQ2 contextual design,
   RQ3 semantic dependency exactness, and RQ4 closure/robustness.
6. **Four contributions:** executable representation; dual semantic
   interfaces; production-separated architecture; validation and evidence.
