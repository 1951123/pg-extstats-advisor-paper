# Evidence policy

1. The paper repository is not the authoritative experiment store.
2. Authoritative experiment artifacts remain in the system or legacy research
   repositories.
3. This repository stores claim-to-evidence links, compact table/figure source
   summaries, exact source commits, and manuscript planning/source.
4. No numerical value is copied into prose without a tracked provenance path.
5. Every reported number must resolve to a tracked artifact and exact commit.
6. Historical experiments must identify their exact legacy source commit or
   tag.
7. Historical artifacts are never rewritten to fit a new narrative.
