\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:evaluation}

This section evaluates five questions.  DMV supplies fixed-design and
multi-realization robustness, Census the authoritative corrected search, and
the clean-room artifact lifecycle evidence.  All use PostgreSQL 16.14, one
base relation, arity-two MCV/FD statistics, a fixed workload, and fixed global
target.  We report q-error objectives, agreement gates, design overlap, and
bounded operation counts, not a physical-per-design wall-clock baseline or
speedup.

\subsection{RQ1 --- Native-Semantic What-If Fidelity}

\paragraph{Question and setup.}
Can the backend-local overlay reproduce the native physical result for a
representable design?  DMV M2.18 compares one 31-statistics design (8 MCV,
23 FD) through hypothetical replay and physical materialization over the same
build, workload, truth, ordinary state, sample, and payload bytes.  M2.35
applies the same gate to 26 deterministic representative configurations,
including empty, singleton, mixed-size, trajectory, random, and
precedence-sensitive designs, including native-absence states.

\paragraph{Metric and result.}
We require equality of the estimate vector, q-error vector, objective, and
selected payloads.  M2.18 is exact for all 1,963 estimates and q-errors, with
31/31 selected payloads matching.  M2.35 is exact for all 26 configurations:
each of its 1,963 estimates and q-errors matches, as do all 396 selected
payload checks and all 1,872 full-repository payload rechecks.  The M2.18
final design reports objective 22,014.061; the empty-design baseline is
42,791.986.  Table~\ref{tab:rq1} lists the gates.

\paragraph{Interpretation and limitation.}

reproduces the native physical result.  This establishes semantic fidelity of
the overlay, not equality after a future native sampling event, cross-version
fidelity, join fidelity, or full-planner fidelity.

\subsection{RQ2 --- State Virtualization and Bounded Evaluation Work}

\paragraph{Question and setup.}
Can one frozen realization evaluate many configurations without per-design
\texttt{ANALYZE}, while query-level reuse preserves exact outcomes?  Census
M2.21 screens the authoritative 4,506-candidate catalog with the
preregistered raw top-5\% rule, then searches the fixed 226-candidate visible
subset; acquisition and shell realization are pre-search operations.

\paragraph{Metric and result.}
The run considered 19,210 conceptual/feasible moves in the fixed
226-candidate subset, pruned 2,441 by the exact bound (12.71\%), performed
16,769 native evaluations, and issued 88,654 planner calls.  A second run
matched the
accepted sequence, final design, objective, cost, counters, and termination
exactly.  The selected design has 112 present candidates (110 MCV and 2 FD),
and the physical validation matched 468/468 estimates and q-errors.

the affected queries.

\subsection{RQ3 --- Contextual Physical Design}

\paragraph{Question and setup.}
Do native statistics interactions make singleton utility insufficient, and can
contextual evaluation construct a maintenance-feasible local design?  DMV
M2.17d uses the complete 72-candidate ADD-only search; Census M2.21 uses the
authoritative screened path (a preregistered raw top-5\% computational screen
over all 4,506 candidates, retaining 226; all retained candidates happened to
be \texttt{PRESENT} in the frozen realization).  The DMV search reached an
ADD-local optimum after 32 rounds.

\paragraph{Metric and result.}
For DMV, the objective fell from 42,791.986 to 22,014.061 (48.56\% relative
improvement), selecting 31 candidates (8 MCV, 23 FD) at modeled cost
175.422.  Twelve accepted DMV candidates had negative singleton utility and
became useful only in context.  For Census, the screened ADD-only run selected
112 candidates (110 MCV, 2 FD), reducing the objective from 5,577.426 to
928.597 (83.35\%) at cost 198.898.  It reached an ADD-local optimum after
113 rounds and repeated exactly.  The singleton observation is therefore
diagnostic evidence of interaction, not a pruning theorem.

that all 4,506 candidates were exhaustively optimized under every move type.

\subsection{RQ4 --- Production-Separated Closure}

\paragraph{Question and setup.}
Can stock PostgreSQL capture support offline advice, DBA-controlled deployment,
and verification without moving the patched backend into production?  M2.33
exercises this in an Ubuntu 24.04 Docker clean-room with a two-candidate,
one-query synthetic fixture: stock capture/production tooling surrounds a
source-built patched PostgreSQL 16.14 advisor.

\paragraph{Metric and result.}
The lifecycle summary is PASS for capture, offline advice, preflight,
deployment, verification, rollback, and the fail-closed corruption,
missing-\texttt{ANALYZE}, wrong-target, and unavailable-capture checks.  Cold
and warm advice produced equal selected design, objective, and recommendation
digests; the warm run hit the cache.  The advisor remained usable while
production was stopped.  This is semantic lifecycle reproducibility, not a
byte-level image or binary reproducibility claim.

\subsection{RQ5 --- Statistics-Realization Robustness}

\paragraph{Question and setup.}
How do fixed and independently selected designs behave across independent DMV
realizations?  M2.19 replays one fixed 31-candidate design across ten fresh
realizations.  M2.20 performs five persisted-sample 72-candidate searches and
a 5$\times$5 cross-evaluation matrix; sample A is the M2.17d lineage and B--E
are independent captures.

\paragraph{Metric and result.}
For M2.19, every run remained beneficial: relative improvement ranged from
43.75\% to 48.62\%; 28 candidates were always present, three changed state,
and none were always absent.  For M2.20, selected design sizes were 31, 31,
31, 28, and 30; pairwise Jaccard similarity ranged from 0.743 to 0.938
(median 0.846).  The consensus core contained 25 candidates and the near-core
contained 28.  Cross-sample local/foreign objective gaps were at most 0.00891\%
and no foreign design beat the local design in the reported matrix.

clean-room lifecycle artifact.
